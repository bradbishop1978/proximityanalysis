Section 1
Privacy Policy

What Stock Pulse is

Stock Pulse is a free inventory tracking tool for convenience store and QSR owners. It runs in your web browser, stores data on your device, and — if you choose to link your phone number — syncs your store data across your devices using secure cloud storage.

The sync feature is completely optional. The app works fully offline without it. We only collect the information described below when you actively choose to use the sync feature.

What we collect

  • Your phone number — only when you choose to link a phone for cross-device sync. Used as your account identifier and to send a one-time verification code (OTP) via SMS. We do not use it for marketing.
  • Your inventory data — item names, stock levels, reorder levels, daily sales rates, prices, and your store name. Only collected if you enable sync. This is your business data; we store it to give it back to you on your other devices.
  • Your franchise/brand selection — the food brand you select from the dropdown (e.g., Krispy Krunchy Chicken). Used only to display the correct branding in the app.
We do not collect your name, email address, location, payment information, or any personal identifiers beyond your phone number. We do not use cookies or analytics trackers.

How we use your information

  • To send a one-time SMS verification code when you set up or restore sync on a device
  • To store your inventory data securely so you can access it from any of your devices
  • We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than making the app work for you

Who stores your data (sub-processors)

We use two third-party services to make sync work:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) — your encrypted inventory data and phone number are stored in Cloudflare's Key-Value (KV) storage. Cloudflare's privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Aircall (a Dialpad company) — used to send the one-time SMS verification code to your phone number. Aircall receives your phone number only to deliver that single message. Aircall's privacy policy: aircall.io/privacy-policy

No other third parties have access to your data.

How long we keep your data

Your synced data is automatically deleted 90 days after your last sync. If you stop using the app, your data disappears on its own — no action needed. Every time you sync, the 90-day clock resets.

You can delete your data at any time before the 90 days are up — see the Contact section below.

Your rights (California residents — CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how we use it (this policy covers that)
  • Delete your personal information — tap "Delete my stored data" in Store Settings (⚙ icon → scroll down), or email us
  • Opt out of sale — we do not sell your personal information to anyone
  • Non-discrimination — exercising any of these rights will not affect your ability to use the app

Children

Stock Pulse is designed for adult business owners. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a minor has submitted information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to how we handle your data, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the sync feature after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
